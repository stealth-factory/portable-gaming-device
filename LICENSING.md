## What the licence lets you do

- **Play, study and change it.** Run it, read the code, change it and build your own versions, for personal use, a hobby, learning, research, or a charity, school or public body.
- **Share it.** Pass on copies and changed versions for non-commercial purposes, with the licence terms (or their link) and the two `Required Notice:` lines at the top of [`LICENSE`](LICENSE).
- **Not for money.** Otherwise, you may not sell it, put it in a paid product or service, run ads on it, or use it to make money in any other way without a commercial licence. If in doubt, ask.
- **Charities, schools and public bodies are covered.** Use by a charitable, educational, public research, public safety or health, environmental or government organisation counts as non-commercial however it's funded, so they don't need a commercial licence.

The full terms are in [`LICENSE`](LICENSE). This summary doesn't replace them.

Apart from the organisations above, to use PGD, or any part of it, commercially (selling a build, publishing it to an app store, putting it in a paid product, running it with ads, or anything else meant to earn money), you need a separate commercial licence from the copyright holder.
